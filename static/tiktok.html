TikTok · Half-yearly · 2019 – 2025

TikTok Government & Legal Requests

TikTok's government & legal request reports, half-yearly since 2019 — a stream distinct from its DSA content-moderation figures: government content-removal requests and requests for user information per country, plus IP/copyright removal requests (global). A tidy-long dataset: each row is one measured value identified by dataset × half-year × country × metric. Rates and percentages are fractions of 1 (never summed), and a global All row sits alongside the per-country rows; every view on this page pins a single dataset and metric.
